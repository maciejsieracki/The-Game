## Change

Added an opt-in `marchMainGroupWhileRendezvous` planner option. The General campaign sets it after its fixed five-turn muster, allowing the leading/assembled force to march toward its selected capital while separated units continue to approach. Ordinary all-in wars retain their existing wait-and-merge behavior. This does not change the muster counter, target selection, or sea eligibility. It does not claim a sea-route validation from this controlled fixture, which changes every tile to land before the campaign begins.

Product touch points:

- `gra/src/game/warAllInCapital.ts`: planner option skips adding the leading group to the rendezvous wait set only when explicitly opted in.
- `gra/src/game/ai.ts`: forwards the option and enables it only in the active General-led attack campaign.

The focused campaign regression in `gra/tools/ai-general-war-campaign-test.cjs` exercises the real `decideAITurn` branch with a four-unit main stack and a separated fifth unit. The planner regression in `gra/tools/war-allin-capital-test.cjs` checks both the main group's capital orders and the follower's rendezvous movement, while existing ordinary all-in assertions continue to require the original wait behavior.
