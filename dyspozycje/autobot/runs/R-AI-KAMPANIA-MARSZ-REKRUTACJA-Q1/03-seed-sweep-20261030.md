## Controlled fixture diagnosis (seed 981044)

At the sixth AI turn, the campaign is `attacking`, `gatherTurnsCompleted=5`; selected target is human capital `city0` at `(5,3)`. The tested General and three seeded stack units are all at `(2,2)`, with 2 movement, not garrisoned or embarked. One other live AI combat unit, `diffbonus_1_1_kbnicqlyz9`, is at `(8,6)` with 2 movement.

The planner diagnostics show five eligible units, `noPath=[]`, and one plan. It orders only the separated unit toward `(6,6)` with mode `rendezvous`; it marks the General and all three co-located troops as `held`. The executor successfully moves the separated unit along `[(7,6),(6,6)]`. It gives no move command to the General stack, so the four tracked units remain at `(2,2)`. The three failed assertions are the missing four same-target move orders, missing 4/4 accepted movements, and unchanged tracked roster position.

Source tracing identifies the immediate behavior: `planWarAllInCapital` makes the main cluster wait when a smaller follower actually moves toward it (`waiting`/`held`, `gra/src/game/warAllInCapital.ts`, around lines 839–849). This means an unrelated/late field unit can delay the five-turn campaign's assembled General stack after the campaign has already switched to `attacking`. It is a real divergence from the requested fixed five-turn muster followed by an attack; it is not a pathfinding `noPath` failure. The controlled scenario is artificial in how it places a test stack, but the separated unit is a live AI unit and the production planner/executor are used. The separate organic scenario passing does not cover this multi-cluster wait condition.

The sea setup in this controlled case is also invalidated: `prepareSeaCorridor` sets a water route, then `beginWarCampaign` rewrites every map tile to `laka`. The post-setup snapshot has 3,952 hexes, all `laka`, zero water. Therefore this particular case cannot be cited as evidence for a sea campaign; the dedicated later sea scenario rebuilds a corridor and passed in the earlier live run.
